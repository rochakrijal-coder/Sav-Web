# Light study prototype

Open index.html directly in a modern browser, or serve the repository with `python3 -m http.server 8080` and visit http://localhost:8080.

No build, packages, external scripts, or hosting subscription required. Canvas computes directional shading from surface normals. Scroll advances a key-light ramp followed by a fill-light ramp; reverse scrolling reverses both. Reduced-motion preferences show the final illuminated state.

The sphere is a material study, not Moon 1 geometry. The uploaded product video is preserved but not integrated in this experiment. There is no beta application or submission backend in this bounded prototype.

Hosting target: static files served by Nginx on Linode after design approval. No deployment has been configured.
